# PhD Thesis — Structural Optimization for Fatigue Resistance

This repository contains my PhD thesis completed at the University of Liège between 2014 and 2018.

**Degree awarded:**  
_Doctorat en Science de l’ingénieur_ (PhD in Engineering Science)

---

## Thesis Overview

This work focuses on structural optimization methods aimed at reducing energy consumption by minimizing structural mass, while explicitly accounting for fatigue resistance. The research is positioned at the intersection of structural mechanics, topology optimization, and fatigue design, with applications to mechanically loaded components.

The thesis extends density-based topology optimization frameworks by revisiting the introduction of stress-based constraints within the design problem. Linear elastic behavior is assumed, and static load cases are used to represent time-varying loading conditions relevant to fatigue design.

A particular emphasis is placed on:
- Stress-constrained topology optimization
- Comparison of stress-based design approaches through standardized test cases
- Identification and mitigation of stress concentrations in optimized structures
- Integration of fatigue resistance criteria into topology optimization loops

The work also explores the design of architectured materials and mesoscale structures, accounting for microstructural geometry.

Fatigue resistance is addressed through a two-stage approach:
1. A static reference load case based on proportional loading assumptions.
2. A more advanced criterion incorporating the Dang Van fatigue model, commonly used in the automotive industry, adapted here for topology optimization.
